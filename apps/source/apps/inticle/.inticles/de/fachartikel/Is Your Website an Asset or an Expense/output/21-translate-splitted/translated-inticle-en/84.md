## Area of responsibility: what the studio can do, and what depends on you

I'll say something my colleagues may not like.

A studio that promises a specific number of leads or a specific ROI from a website either does not understand what it is talking about or is knowingly misleading you.

A website's ROI depends on four variables:
1/ The quality of the website itself (studio).
2/ The volume and quality of traffic (advertising, SEO, reputation).
3/ The attractiveness of the offer (product, price, terms).
4/ The quality of lead handling (speed, professionalism).

The studio controls one quarter of the equation. Promising results without controlling 75% of the variables is either naive or manipulative.

What the studio can guarantee:
- Technical reliability and speed.
- Properly configured analytics.
- Handover of ownership rights and access credentials.
- Doc and training.
- Ongoing support according to an agreed process.

Everything else is an area of shared responsibility.
