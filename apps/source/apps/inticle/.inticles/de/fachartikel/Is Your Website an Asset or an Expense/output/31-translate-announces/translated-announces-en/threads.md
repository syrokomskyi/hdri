Many companies have websites that look solid. But that alone does not make them an asset.

The criterion is fairly strict: if you stopped advertising tomorrow, would the website continue to deliver value - or would it remain just a nice-looking page?

I broke down this difference as a systemic issue rather than a design one. It touches on analytics, access control, and why a website i.e. not integrated into business processes rarely creates real value.

I also addressed the topic of AI separately: if a site's structure is unreadable for machines, the business gradually loses visibility in a new channel.

A website as an artifact is one scenario. A website as infrastructure is a completely different one.
