## Continuity and Digital Independence

There is a specific aspect for German SMEs.

Many craft businesses and family-owned companies face the challenge of business succession. What deters potential successors? *Inhaberrisiko* — dependence on the owner’s personality.

A company where customers come through the boss’s personal connections is valued lower than a company with established acquisition channels. A functioning website with a measurable funnel is a way to demonstrate the business’s independence. This directly affects valuation in a succession scenario.

Then there is the GDPR and other requirements. A website that creates legal risks is not simply an inefficient expense. It is a potential threat. Proper privacy configuration is not a formality, but basic hygiene.
