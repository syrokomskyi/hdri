Usually, everything comes down to two scenarios.
Either you sell time. Or you come in through capital.

But there is another question.
Is participation through what has been created possible-when a stake arises not from money, but from completed work.

I.e. what I am reflecting on in this text. Not in a romantic sense, but in a constructive one.
Where this approach has a foundation. Where it is legally fragile. Where it can easily slide into aesthetically packaged unpaid labor.

The key distinction is between Werk and Wirkung.
Between an asset that retains its form, and an effect that exists only in motion.

What interests me is not slogans, but the boundaries of the model.
Because without honestly naming the risks, any "new form of participation" quickly loses its moral right to exist.
