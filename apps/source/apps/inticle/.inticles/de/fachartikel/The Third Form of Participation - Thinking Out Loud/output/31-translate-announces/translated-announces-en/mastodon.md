I'm thinking about a "third form of participation" between salary and capital. Not through selling time. Not through money up front. But through what has been created.

In the text, I distinguish between Werk and Wirkung: what can exist as a separate asset, and what exists only as an effect over time. This is precisely where the line runs between fair participation and unpaid labor given a nice name.
