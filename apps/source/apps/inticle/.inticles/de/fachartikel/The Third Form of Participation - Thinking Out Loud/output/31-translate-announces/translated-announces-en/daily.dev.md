I examine a participation model in which contribution is recognized not through salary and not through an equity stake tied to capital, but through a completed digital artifact. The key framework is the distinction between Werk (an asset with its own state and a long lifespan) and Wirkung (an effect over time i.e. difficult to attribute and to scale without conflicts).

The text contains not only the idea, but also its constraints: legal fragility, dependence on sales, and the risk of replacing the model with unpaid labor. Its practical value lies in the very architecture used to assess contribution.
