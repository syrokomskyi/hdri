## Why this matters right now

Working hypothesis: the wage-based model is under pressure. Selling time is becoming a less reliable strategy. Especially in design and software development, where AI is starting to automate routine tasks. This is not a fact. It is my sense of the trend.

If this hypothesis is correct, then when the wage-based model constrains possibilities, what will remain is capital (for those who have money) and created value (for those who have a stake). Right now, I am trying to build the infrastructure for the latter. Not as a universal replacement, but as a niche experiment for work that can become a finished asset with a long life.

The model is honest about its limits: "not a replacement for social protection", "not suitable for making money quickly". But it is precisely these limits that make it accessible only to those who already have financial security.

This is a contradiction that has not yet been resolved in practice.

Perhaps this will remain a small-scale practice within a single studio. I.e. also fine. But it seems important to keep trying while there is still room for mistakes. Because if the anxiety about the future of wage labor proves justified, we will need new forms of participation. And it is better to experiment now than to wait until it becomes a necessity.
