## Honest risks

I acknowledge the asymmetry of risk. The author invests time and life without guarantees. The studio also commits resources-infrastructure, engineering time, sales. But the author's risk is more direct and more painful.

**No baseline income.** The model does not replace social protection. A person running a Werkstück often cannot simultaneously hold a full-time job elsewhere. They end up in a zone of prolonged unpaid work with no guarantee that revenue will ever appear.

This filters participants by privilege. A financial cushion, a partner with income, savings… We risk selecting not the most capable, but those who can afford to wait. Without addressing this, we reproduce the class-based barrier to entry that we claim to oppose.

**Legal risk in Germany.** In the draft agreement, I am leaning toward a Werkvertrag. But German regulators look at the actual characteristics of the arrangement. Integration into the workshop, discipline, acceptance control-all of this can be interpreted as concealed subordination (Scheinselbständigkeit). This has to be assessed at the level of the actual organization of work.

**Deferred revenue may never materialize.** Some Werkstück may never generate money. If 1 out of 10 sells, then 9 authors worked for free. I.e. not fraud if everything has been disclosed honestly. But it is an asymmetric risk.

**The difficulty of attribution for Wirkung.** How do you measure the contribution to a sale, support, or customer acquisition? Wirkung is the most vulnerable point for conflicts and a sense of unfairness. It requires shorter, more transparent, and more cautious mechanics than Werk.

**Dependence on the studio.** Even if the thing was demonstrably created by you, its value depends entirely on a sale to which you may have no access. This is a structural asymmetry. The model does not yet fully resolve it.
