## BRAND VISUAL PROFILE: WEBGOGOL

**1. Core Aesthetic (The Vibe):**
Organic Craftsman meets Contemporary Gallery Minimalism. The mood is grounded, reliable, tactile, and warm. It should feel like high-end, meticulously crafted physical objects rather than digital or high-tech concepts.

**2. Strict Color Palette (Translated from UI):**

* **Base/Background:** Warm off-white, creamy ivory, or soft parchment (equivalent to `#fffcf7`). Absolutely no cold or sterile white.
* **Primary Dark:** Deep Forest Green (`#1a4332`).
* **Primary Warm:** Matte Terracotta / Burnt Clay (`#b54c1f`).
* **Accent:** Warm Gold / Amber (`#d69e2e`).
* *Negative Constraint:* ABSOLUTELY NO blue, cyan, magenta, cyberpunk neons, or cold industrial greys.

**3. Materiality (Physical equivalents of the UI):**

* **For "Forest Green":** Dark polished stone, malachite, green oxidized copper, or dark green matte ceramic.
* **For "Terracotta":** Raw unglazed clay, warm matte terracotta, or rich mahogany wood.
* **For "Gold/Accent":** Brushed warm brass, amber resin, or a subtle internal warm/golden light source.
* **For "Glassmorphism" (UI Blur):** Thick frosted glass, translucent acrylic blocks, or soft optical refractions.

**4. Lighting & Environment:**
Soft, warm cinematic studio lighting. Diffused architectural caustics. Shadows must be soft and carry a warm earthy tint (no pure harsh black shadows). The objects must be placed in a vast, empty, warm off-white studio environment to emphasize negative space (70% empty space).
