# Analytical frame

Turn the approved evidence corpus into a synthesis, uncertainty model, and knowledge-gap framing that editorial writing can safely build on, including routes that must transform raw project evidence such as Git history into narrative episodes.

## Entry criteria

- The active evidence route is already materialized, whether through discovery/acquisition or operator payload normalization.

## Steps

- 5. Normalize operator-prepared payload `normalize-operator-payload`
- 6. Build analytical synthesis `analytical-synthesis`
- 7. Confirm Analysis Frame Phase `wait-phase-analysis-frame-accepted`

## Success signals

- Synthesis and knowledge gap artifacts exist.
- The operator can evaluate the intended narrative frame before drafting starts to amplify it, especially when raw evidence needs to become case-study structure instead of chronology.
- Any operator answers that close or narrow knowledge gaps are written into `phase-analysis-frame-accepted.md` and become part of the editorial handoff.

## Exit criteria

- The analytical frame is approved for editorial drafting and no longer treated as provisional research output.
- Downstream editorial gogols can rely on the approval note as the latest human clarification layer for framing, caveats, and unresolved questions.
