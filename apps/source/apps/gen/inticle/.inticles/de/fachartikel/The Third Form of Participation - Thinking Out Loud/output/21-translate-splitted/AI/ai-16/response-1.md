## Werk and Wirkung: two natures of contribution

In this model, I distinguish between two natures of contribution. Two types of value with different mechanics.

**Werk — that which has a state.** A product, asset, object with coordinates and integrity. Evidentiary in the moment: “here it is, in this condition, with my signature.” Werk accumulates. A design system continues to live on and generate value after its creation.

In German copyright law, there is a logic of the author’s fair participation in the success of the work. If a Werk truly is a protected work, a share in it may have legal grounds. But not every Werkstück is automatically a work in the legal sense. That must be assessed separately.

**Wirkung — that which has an effect over time.** Facilitation, influence, flow. Sales, customer acquisition, infrastructure support. Evidentiary only through dynamics: “here is what changed over the period.” Wirkung fades. The effect dissipates. A customer brought in remains with the studio years later because of the studio, not because of the person who once brought them in.

Wirkung does not create a protected work. A share based on Wirkung cannot rely on copyright law. This is not a deprivation. It is the truth about the nature of the contribution: where there is no accumulation, it is harder to justify a perpetual share.
