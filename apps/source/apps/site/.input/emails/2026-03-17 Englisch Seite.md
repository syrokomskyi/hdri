In order to develop permanent local access to basic health care, in 2016 and 2017, fourteen local volunteers called "brigadistas" in eight mountain villages were trained by professional health care providers to diagnose common illnesses and administer basic medications.   
These brigadistas are in close communication with our local team in Ocotal, get support by doctor and nurse and inform us about the patients in the communities.

**Girls Day Shelter Maria de Nazareth**  
   
Nicaragua Project also supported the association MARIA de NAZARETH in Ocotal, which cared for girls from extremely poor problem families. The girls' backgrounds may include alcohol, drugs, prostitution and sexual abuse.   
We recently changed our way to work and support now single families, emphasizing the importance of education, organize apprenticships, and offer university scholarships.  
   
   
   
**The NGO and Its History**  
   
Nicaragua Projekt is a non-profit association which supports medical and social projects in northern Nicaragua.  
   
The commitment of Dr. Katrin Hennings, a pediatrician, and Reinhart Bein, a retired pilot, in the Ocotal region began in 2005, when they established a monthly mobile clinic on behalf of the organization German Doctors eV. Through this work, they got to know Dulce Maria Calderon and Viola Castillo and their project Maria de Nazareth, a Girls' Day Shelter established in 1999\.   
   
In 2006, Katrin and Reinhart included other volunteer supporters to found the non-profit organization Nicaragua Projekt e.V. in order to support the Girls Day Shelter Maria de Nazareth and other social activities. Following the withdrawal of German Doctors e.V. in 2015, the mobile clinic was taken over by Nicaragua Projekt e.V.
